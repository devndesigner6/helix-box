HELIXBOX · GETTING STARTED

From laptop
to phone.

Open your existing project from Android. Your laptop keeps running the files, tools, and processes you already use.

CLI Guide

Run the CLI
Open a terminal inside your project and run npx helixbox-cli. Keep the CLI running and your laptop connected to the internet.

Pair the Android app
Download HelixBox from GitHub Releases, then scan the QR shown by the CLI.

Return to your workspace
Use the saved connection or Past Sessions while the laptop session is valid. Generate a new QR only through the new-session flow when you need a new connection.

Get your agents ready
Install and authenticate Codex, OpenCode, Claude Code, or Hermes on the laptop. The app reports availability from that machine's CLI status.
